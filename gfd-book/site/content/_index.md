A graduate GFD course where **every model runs in your browser**. The text
derives each minimal model; the embedded [marimo](https://marimo.io) notebook
lets you turn the knob and watch the balance break — no installation required.
Symbols and sign conventions are fixed on the [notation]({{< relref "notation.md" >}}) page.
