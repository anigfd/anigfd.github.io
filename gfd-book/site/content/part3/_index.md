The workhorse approximation of the whole subject. Small-Rossby-number
flow is captured almost entirely by conserving one scalar, the
**quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity**. From there: **Rossby waves**
and the ray theory that bends them into great circles, and the exact
decomposition of a two-layer fluid into independent **barotropic** and
**baroclinic** modes.
