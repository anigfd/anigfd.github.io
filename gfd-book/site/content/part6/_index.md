From an unstable jet to the general circulation. **Geostrophic
turbulence** cascades energy upscale into coherent vortices and, on a
rotating planet, zonal jets; the same eddies set an **effective
diffusivity** for any tracer they stir. Closes with the large-scale
response to steady forcing: **wind-driven gyres** and their western
boundary currents, and **buoyancy-driven overturning** circulation.
