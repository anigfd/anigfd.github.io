Vertical structure and internal waves. A continuously stratified fluid
decomposes into **normal modes**, each an independent QG system with its
own deformation radius — the two-layer model is the crudest possible
truncation of this richer theory. **Internal gravity waves** carry energy
perpendicular to their own phase propagation; and a wave's presence
leaves a permanent, measurable mark on the mean flow it rides, from
Stokes drift to critical-layer absorption.
