Where the energy for storms and eddies actually comes from. **Barotropic
instability** draws on horizontal shear; **baroclinic instability** — the
single most consequential instability in this subject — draws on the
potential energy locked in a sloping temperature surface. Closes with the
faster, more local mechanisms that matter at fronts and in stratified
shear layers: symmetric, inertial, and Kelvin–Helmholtz instability.
